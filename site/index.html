Arabic Spoken Dialect Identification

Objective

Classify spoken Arabic into one of 11 country-level dialects under cross-domain conditions, where the test set differs in domain and source from training and development data. For domain generalization, a secondary-domain development set is provided alongside the primary development data.

The label space is the full 20-class inventory and admits no abstention: a system scores over all twenty, while the blind set covers only a subset.

Method

How it was built.

The blind domain is unknown at development time, so in-domain accuracy measures fit to the one domain the test set is known not to come from. Every decision below is made on out-of-domain accuracy instead.

01Literature review

NADI ran as a text benchmark from 2020 and moved to speech in 2025, where ELYADATA won with a Whisper-large-v3 encoder and attentive pooling at 79.8%. Robustness has been pursued separately — voice conversion to normalise speaker identity, augmentation-based domain diversification. What none of it settles is how well a development-time proxy predicts blind cross-domain performance.

02Data, preprocessing and augmentation

ADI20-micro
67,015 training and 10,806 validation clips over 20 classes, balanced by duration rather than clip count.
Casablanca
8 of the 20 classes, split at the program boundary rather than the clip boundary, since clips from one program share a speaker and a recording chain. 10,899 clips join training; the rest is held out.
MADIS-5
Five macro-regions, never trained on — the only genuinely out-of-domain measurement in the system.

Audio is decoded to 16 kHz mono with no voice-activity detection, loudness normalisation or transcript filtering. Each batch is cropped to one duration drawn from {1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 12} seconds — the main robustness measure, since blind clip lengths are unknown — with SpecAugment applied on top.

03Modelling

Three backbones, one architecture: encoder, mask-aware mean pooling, 20-way linear head.

The criteria disagree: ADI-whisper-ADI20 leads on ADI20 by 5.6 points and trails on MADIS-5 by 32, so in-domain accuracy would have rejected the encoder that generalises best. LoRA collapses both backbones to about 16% on 20-way Casablanca, so the representation has to be adapted by full fine-tuning.

Fine-tuning uses ADI20-micro plus the Casablanca training portion, resampled by count(y)−0.5. The head trains alone for 500 steps, then the encoder unfreezes with a linear re-warm-up; learning rates 2×10−3 and 2×10−5 on cosine decay, batch 128, layer-mixing, 6,000 steps. At inference each clip is passed at full length, mean-pooled, and assigned the argmax over twenty logits.

Result

52.51% on the blind test set. Fourth place.
