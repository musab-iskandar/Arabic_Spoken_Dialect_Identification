Method

The hard part was never the accuracy.

The model fits its training corpus comfortably and generalises far worse out of domain — which is exactly what the hidden test set is. Everything here exists to attack that gap.

01

Fine-tune

An Arabic speech encoder is fine-tuned for 20-way dialect. The cues that identify a dialect sit in the middle layers, not just the last one, so every layer is weighted.

02

Split honestly

The out-of-domain corpus is split by program, not by clip. One half picks the checkpoint. The other half never influences that choice, so its score is not measured on the clips that produced it.

03

Keep it stable

One optimizer, built once, with a learning-rate schedule that never restarts mid-run. That is most of what kept long runs from drifting.

Result

The gap closed, then inverted.
